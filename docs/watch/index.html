Scope and production

This is real guest footage from the experimental N00/QEMU port, using a PR1.0-era kernel and adaptation layer with PR1.3 retail userspace. It does not demonstrate complete N9 hardware emulation or compatibility with every application.

The edited video is 3840 × 2160 at 60 fps, composing the original 480 × 864 guest surface inside a fixed N9 frame. These production settings are not a measurement of guest or original-device display performance. The instrumental score was composed and synthesized in code with GPT and Codex assistance, without sampled songs or narration.

N9 artwork in the video: Liwen Guo / Livven, using the creator’s local version with logo and glass gloss hidden. Thanks to QEMU, the Nokia N00/DGLES and Harmattan developers, the preservation community, GPT and Codex.
